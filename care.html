So You Want a Malamute

Congratulations. You have chosen a 90-pound opinion with fur.

Basic Malamute Survival

  • Exercise them regularly unless you'd like them to renovate your house themselves.
  • Brush them often. It won't help, but at least you'll feel like you're doing something.
  • Keep food out of reach. Then move it farther. No, farther than that.
  • Train them consistently. They understand you. Whether they care is a separate issue.

Accept Your New Reality

Your clothes will have fur. Your furniture will have fur. Food you haven't even purchased yet somehow already has fur.

In exchange, you get a loyal, ridiculous, dramatic best friend who is absolutely certain this is their house and you're just lucky enough to live in it.
